About HanVerse

Mandarin, made joyful — for learners ages 5–12.

Why we exist

Mandarin is one of the most useful languages a child can learn — and one of the easiest to give up on. Textbooks are intimidating, apps for adults are boring for kids, and progress feels invisible. HanVerse flips that: every lesson is a small win, every character has a story, and a friendly panda named HanPan celebrates every step.

How learning works

1. Placement. A 2-minute quiz finds your child's level — total beginner or already reading characters — so they start exactly where they belong.

2. Roadmap. A clear path through the official YCT standards (Youth Chinese Test levels 1–4), lesson by lesson, with a "next lesson" marker that always shows what's now.

3. Lessons that feel like play. Vocabulary, stories, audio from native pronunciation, and quizzes — with XP, streaks and leagues that make practice a habit.

4. Practice that sticks. Words your child meets in lessons feed a smart review deck, so nothing learned is forgotten.

5. Parents stay in the loop. A dedicated parents' dashboard shows progress, streaks, XP and activity — no guessing.

What's inside

Four YCT-aligned worlds (YCT 1–4), games, stories, daily challenges, a speaking practice space, achievements, and HanPan — whose reactions, sounds and confetti turn studying into something kids ask to do.

Where we're based

HanVerse is built in Nairobi, Kenya, for families everywhere who want their children to grow up at home in more than one language.
