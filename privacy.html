2. What we collect

Account information

Name, username, email address, password (stored only as a salted hash), role, chosen level and account settings.

Learning information

Lessons opened, answers given, quiz and game results, streaks, daily goals, achievements and placement results.

Family and classroom information

Where a guardian links a child: the child's display name, year group, the guardian's controls and goals, and any assignments or messages the guardian or a teacher sends.

Payment information

Plan, subscription status and the last four digits of a card. Full card details are handled by Stripe and never reach our servers.

Technical information

IP address, browser and device type, and error and security logs, including records of administrative actions taken in the console.

3. Children's information

HanVerse is used by children, so this section matters most.

  • A learner under 13 does not create an account directly. A parent or guardian creates the account and remains responsible for it.
  • We collect only what a lesson needs: a display name, year group, and learning activity. We do not ask a child for an address, a phone number, a photograph or a real full name.
  • We do not provide messaging between learners. A child cannot contact another child through HanVerse.
  • Classroom participation, including whether a child's display name is visible to a class, is off unless a guardian turns it on, and can be turned off again at any time.
  • We do not use a child's information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

A guardian can review, correct or delete a child's information from the parent hub, or by writing to CONTACT EMAIL.

4. Why we use it

  • To create and secure accounts, and to sign you in.
  • To deliver lessons, games and progress tracking.
  • To let a guardian or teacher see the progress they are responsible for.
  • To take payment and manage subscriptions and trials.
  • To send service email such as verification, password reset and trial reminders.
  • To keep the Service secure, diagnose faults and prevent abuse.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not send marketing email to a child's address, and we do not send marketing email to an adult unless they have asked for it.

5. Our legal bases

Where the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (delivering the Service you signed up for); consent (optional features, and a guardian's consent for a child's account); our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement) balanced against your rights; and legal obligation (tax and accounting records).

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies, the equivalent bases are contract, consent, legitimate interests and legal obligation.

6. Who we share it with

We share information only with processors that help us run the Service, and only as far as they need it:

  • Neon — managed PostgreSQL database.
  • Netlify — website hosting and serverless functions.
  • Render — realtime classroom service.
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management.
  • Brevo — transactional email delivery.
  • Google Fonts — typeface delivery.
  • AI providers — lesson help and explanation features, which receive the question text you submit and not your account identity.

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect a child or another person from harm.

11. Security

Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Traffic is encrypted in transit. Administrative access requires an elevated session and is recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password and tell us at once if you suspect a problem.
