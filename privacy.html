What we collect

  • Account details: username, email, password (hashed), full name, age.
  • Learning data: lessons completed, quiz answers, XP, streaks, review-queue words, game scores.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Speech (optional): if you use Speak Aloud, audio is processed by your browser on your device and is not stored by us.
  • Technical data: IP address for rate limiting and security.

Children's privacy (important)

Hanverse is intended for children ages 5–12. We collect only what is needed to run the service. For learners under the age of digital consent in their country (e.g. under 13 in the US, under 13–16 in parts of Europe), we require a parent or guardian to set up or approve the account. Parents may contact us at [contact email] to review, correct, or delete their child's personal information at any time. Learning data is used to run the product — it is not sold and is not used to advertise to children.

Who we share data with

  • Neon — database hosting
  • Netlify — website hosting
  • Stripe — payments
  • Brevo — email delivery
  • OpenAI — text-to-speech generation (pre-generated audio, not learner audio)

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep data

Account and learning data are kept while the account is active. Deleting an account removes its personal data (see "Deleting your account").

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your data: [contact email] or self-service deletion.
  • Parents: email [contact email] from the address on the account.
  • Marketing emails include an unsubscribe link.

Deleting your account

You can request deletion at [contact email], or self-serve if available in Settings. Deletion removes account and learning data from our systems.
