Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

HanVerse ("we", "us") operates this Mandarin-learning service for children. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Who we are

HanVerse, Nairobi, Kenya. Privacy and children's-data requests: privacy@hanverse.com. Support: support@hanverse.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email, password (hashed), full name, age.
  • Learning data: lessons completed, quiz answers, XP, streaks, review-queue words, game scores.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Panda AI Tutor chats (optional): if your child chats with Panda — including before signing in, from the signup or login pages — the message text is sent to an AI provider to generate each reply. Conversations are held only in temporary server memory (the last 20 messages, to keep context); they are not stored in our database and disappear when the visit ends or the service restarts, so we cannot show parents chat logs. Panda's instructions forbid it from asking for personal details, encouraging children to keep secrets from parents, or discussing unsafe content, and the chat is rate-limited to prevent abuse. Chat content is never used to advertise to children.
  • Speech (optional): in Speak Aloud and pronunciation practice, audio is processed by your browser on your device; we do not store learner audio. Only the pass/fail result of a pronunciation check reaches our servers.
  • Technical data: IP address for rate limiting and security.

Children's privacy (important)

HanVerse is intended for children ages 5–12. We collect only what is needed to run the service. For learners under the age of digital consent in their country (e.g. under 13 in the US, under 13–16 in parts of Europe), we require a parent or guardian to set up or approve the account — a child cannot sign up alone. Parents may contact us at privacy@hanverse.com to review, correct, or delete their child's personal information at any time. Learning data is used to run the product — it is not sold and is not used to advertise to children. We apply the same standard in Kenya under the Data Protection Act, 2019, which treats a child's data as special.

Who we share data with

  • Neon — database hosting
  • Netlify — website and API hosting
  • Stripe — payments (card data never touches our systems)
  • Brevo — email delivery
  • OpenAI — pre-generated lesson audio (text-to-speech) and the Panda AI Tutor's replies (learner chat text)
  • Google — if you use "Continue with Google", Google shares your account name and email with us to create your account
  • Render — hosts the live pronunciation/practice room service

We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep data

Account and learning data are kept while the account is active. If an account is unused for 24 months we delete it and its learning data. Panda chat text is not persisted at all — it lives only in temporary memory during a visit (see above). If a parent deletes a child's account, the account and learning data are removed from our systems immediately, and from encrypted backups within 30 days.

Your choices

  • Access, correct, or delete your data: privacy@hanverse.com, or delete your account yourself in Settings.
  • Parents: email privacy@hanverse.com from the address on the account.
  • Marketing emails include an unsubscribe link.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account yourself in Settings → Delete account, or request deletion at privacy@hanverse.com and we will do it within 30 days. Deletion removes account and learning data from our systems; see "How long we keep data" for backup timing.

Security

Passwords are hashed; connections are encrypted; administrative access is restricted and logged.

Changes

We will post updates on this page with a new date. Material changes to children's data will be announced to parents by email where we have it.
