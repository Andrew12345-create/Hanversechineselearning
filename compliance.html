Child Data Protection & Compliance

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Read alongside our Privacy Policy, Terms and Cookie Policy.

Who this covers

HanVerse is a Chinese-learning platform used by students, including children under 18, together with their parents or guardians, teachers and administrators. Because we hold information about children, this page explains the specific protections that apply to that information. It is written for parents and guardians first.

We act as a data controller for the account and learning information described here. We handle it under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, the Data Protection (General) Regulations, 2021, and applicable ODPC guidance on children's data.

What we collect, and why

  • Account basics — a name or display name, an age band, and sign-in credentials. Needed to create and secure the account.
  • Parent or guardian contact — name and email. Needed to verify consent and to reach the responsible adult.
  • Learning information — lessons opened, quiz results, streaks, mastery and assignment completion. Needed to show progress and to set the next lesson.
  • Classroom membership — the class code a student joins and the assignments set for that class. Needed for teacher-led use.
  • Payment references — a transaction reference from Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.
  • Technical logs — IP address and request metadata, kept briefly for security and abuse prevention.

We do not collect home addresses, live location, social media accounts or government identifiers. We do not collect photographs or voice recordings unless a parent or guardian turns on the matching optional permission described below.

Parental consent

An account for a child under 13 is created through a parent or guardian, not by the child alone. Consent is recorded against the specific purpose, the version of the notice in force at the time, and the date. Withdrawing consent is timestamped and the original grant is kept, so there is a full history of what was agreed and when.

Consent is split so that optional permissions are never bundled into the essential service:

  • Essential service — the account, lessons and progress. Required for the platform to work, and cannot be withdrawn while the account is active.
  • Photographs — optional, off by default.
  • Audio recording — optional, off by default. Pronunciation practice runs in the browser and is not stored unless this is enabled.
  • AI tutor — optional, off by default. Covers sending lesson questions to the AI helper.
  • Service email — optional, off by default. Covers progress summaries and product news. Account and security email is separate and always sent.

A parent or guardian can review, grant or withdraw any optional permission from the Parent Hub. Withdrawal takes effect for future processing; it does not retroactively delete records we are required to keep.

Access, correction and deletion

A parent or guardian can request a copy of their child's information, ask for a correction, or ask for the account to be deleted. Deletion requests are queued, reviewed and resolved with a recorded outcome and the identity of the reviewer, so a request cannot quietly disappear. Where a legal retention obligation applies — for example a payment record — that specific record is kept for its required period and the rest is removed.

To make a request, email andrewmunamwangi@gmail.com from the address on the account, or use the request option in the Parent Hub.

How long we keep it

Each category has a defined retention period rather than being kept because storage is cheap. Learning records are kept while the account is active and for a short period afterwards so a returning family is not starting from nothing. Security logs are kept briefly. Payment references are kept for the period tax and accounting rules require. When a period ends, the data is removed from active systems and from backups on the backup schedule.

Safeguarding and online safety

  • Student profiles are not publicly visible. There is no public student directory.
  • There is no student-to-student private messaging. Classroom features are scoped to a class a teacher owns.
  • Leaderboards show a display name only, and a student can be removed from them by a parent or guardian.
  • A parent or guardian can revoke classroom participation for their child at any time.

Security

Passwords are stored only as a slow hash, never in plain text. Traffic is served over HTTPS. Access to another person's records is checked on the server for every request, not hidden in the interface. Administrative actions are logged. An administrator can sign out every active session on an account if it is believed to be compromised, and administrator sign-in supports a time-based one-time code as a second factor.

AI features

The AI tutor is optional and off unless a parent or guardian enables it. When it is used, the lesson question and the minimum context needed to answer it are sent to the model provider. We do not send the child's name, email or account identifier. AI answers are learning aids, not assessments, and are not used to grade a student.

If something goes wrong

We monitor unusual sign-in activity, privileged administrative actions and abnormal access to student records. If a breach occurs that creates a real risk of harm, we notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, and we notify affected parents and guardians without undue delay. Every incident is recorded with what happened, what was affected and what was changed as a result.

Complaints

If you are not satisfied with how we have handled your child's information, contact us first at andrewmunamwangi@gmail.com so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

Changes

If we change how we handle children's data, we update this page and the version recorded against new consent. Material changes are communicated to parents and guardians before they take effect.
