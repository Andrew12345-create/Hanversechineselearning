Refund Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Part of our Terms of Service.

Free trial

The 7-day trial is free and needs no payment card — it never auto-converts into a paid plan, so you can't be charged for forgetting it. If you subscribed yourself, cancel any time in Settings → Subscription; access continues until the end of the period you paid for.

Subscriptions

Paid subscriptions renew monthly via Stripe. You can cancel any time in Settings → Subscription; access continues until the end of the paid period. We do not refund partial periods unless required by law, except as below.

When we refund

  • Duplicate or incorrect charges — refunded in full.
  • Technical faults that stop the service working for 7+ days and that we cannot fix — refunded pro-rata.
  • First-time subscribers who did not intend to subscribe — refunded on request within 14 days of the charge, at our discretion.

How to ask

Email support@hanverse.com with the email on the account and the charge date. We reply within 5 business days. Refunds go back to the original payment method. Statutory consumer rights (including EU/UK 14-day withdrawal rights) are unaffected.
