1. In short

  • The free tier costs nothing, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Trials are card-free and never auto-renew, so a trial never creates a charge.
  • A paid plan can be refunded in full within REFUND WINDOW of the first payment.
  • After that window, cancel to stop future charges; the current period is not refunded.

2. First payment

If you are not satisfied with a paid plan, write to CONTACT EMAIL within REFUND WINDOW of the first payment and we will refund it in full to the original payment method.

We may ask which account the payment relates to so we can find the charge.

3. Renewals

Renewal payments are not refundable once the period has begun, except where the law requires it or where the Service was unavailable for a significant part of the period through our fault. In that case we will refund the affected period pro rata.

Cancelling stops the next renewal. It does not end access early: you keep the plan until the end of the period you have paid for.

4. Duplicate or mistaken charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, charged after cancelling, or charged an amount you did not agree to, contact us and we will correct it. Duplicate charges are refunded in full.

5. How to request a refund

  1. Email CONTACT EMAIL from the address on the account.
  2. Include the account email and the date and amount of the payment.
  3. Tell us briefly what went wrong, so we can fix it.

We aim to reply within RESPONSE WINDOW. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method; your bank or card issuer controls how long it takes to appear, typically 5–10 business days.

6. Your statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits a right you have under consumer law where you live, including any right to cancel a distance contract within a statutory cooling-off period.
