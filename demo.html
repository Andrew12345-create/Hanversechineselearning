YCT 1 · Greetings · Step 1 of 5

你好

nǐ hǎo
hello
Say it: “nee how”. 你 (nǐ) means you and 好 (hǎo) means good — together they are the everyday hello.
YCT 1 · Greetings · Step 2 of 5

谢谢

xiè xie
thank you
Say it: “shyeh shyeh”. The second syllable is light and quick — Chinese often softens a repeated syllable like this.
YCT 1 · Greetings · Step 3 of 5

再见

zài jiàn
goodbye
Say it: “dzai jyen”. Literally “again see” — see you again.
