Cookie Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Part of our Privacy Policy.

What we use

HanVerse keeps you signed in with browser local storage (your sign-in token and basic profile). We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers, or cross-site tracking cookies.

Because the sign-in token lives in your browser, we harden the site against cross-site scripting with a strict Content-Security-Policy header on every response, and the token is removed from the browser the moment you log out. We are moving sign-in to an httpOnly cookie, which browsers hide entirely from page scripts, as an early follow-up.

What this means

  • Sign-in storage: strictly necessary — keeps you logged in. Cleared when you log out or clear your browser data.
  • Preference storage: remembers choices like sound settings.

Third parties on this site

Stripe may set cookies during checkout for fraud prevention (governed by Stripe's privacy policy). We embed no advertising networks.

Control

You can clear local storage any time in your browser settings; you'll just need to sign in again. Questions: andrewmunamwangi@gmail.com.
