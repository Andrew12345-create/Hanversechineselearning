1. What this covers

This policy covers cookies and the browser storage HanVerse uses, including localStorage and service-worker caches. It should be read with our Privacy Policy.

2. What we use

Strictly necessary

  • Sign-in state — stored in localStorage under hanverse_user and hanverse_token so you stay signed in between pages. Removing it signs you out.
  • Theme and preferences — your chosen theme and reminder settings.
  • Offline cache — a service worker stores pages and assets so the app opens when the connection drops.

Not used

  • No advertising cookies.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking pixels.
  • No cross-site profiling, and no tracking of a child's activity for marketing.

3. Third parties

Stripe may set its own cookies on its payment pages to process a payment and prevent fraud. Google Fonts is requested as a stylesheet and does not set a cookie, but the request does reach Google's servers. Those services are governed by their own policies.

4. How to control them

  • Sign out to clear the sign-in entries.
  • Clear site data in your browser settings to remove everything HanVerse has stored.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser; strictly necessary storage is required for sign-in, so blocking it will stop you signing in.

Because we set no advertising or analytics cookies, there is no consent banner to dismiss.

5. Changes

If we add a category of cookie, we will update this page and, where consent is required, ask for it before the cookie is set.
