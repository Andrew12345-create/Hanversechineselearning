Frequently Asked Questions ❓

Everything you need to know about Hanverse.

What age is Hanverse for? ▼

Hanverse is designed for children aged 5-12. Our content is tailored to be age-appropriate and engaging for young learners.

How much does Hanverse cost? ▼

We offer a free tier with 10+ lessons and games. Premium is $9.99/month with full access to all content, AI tutor, and progress tracking.

Can I use Hanverse offline? ▼

Currently, Hanverse requires an internet connection. Offline mode is coming soon as a premium feature!

Is Hanverse safe for my child? ▼

Absolutely! Hanverse is kid-safe with no ads, no inappropriate content, and our AI is specifically designed for young learners. Parents have full control over their child's account.

What curriculum does Hanverse follow? ▼

Hanverse follows the official YCT (Youth Chinese Test) curriculum from YCT 1 to YCT 4. Our lessons prepare students for certification.
