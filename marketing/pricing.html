Frequently asked

Pricing questions, answered

Can my child try HanVerse before I pay?
Yes. The Starter plan is free forever and includes a full starter lesson set, so you can judge the quality — and your child can judge the fun — before spending anything.
How do I cancel or switch plans?
Both are two clicks from your account settings. When you downgrade you keep access until the end of the current billing month, then return to Starter.
Do you offer a discount for classrooms or schools?
We do — for licensed teachers and schools. Contact us with your details and we will put together a tailored plan within two business days.
Is my payment information safe?
Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe, the same provider used by millions of businesses worldwide. HanVerse never sees or stores your card details.
What happens if we pause for a few months?
Nothing bad. Your account and progress stay intact. When you return, your child picks up exactly where they left off.
