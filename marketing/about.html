Our mission

Learning a language should feel like play, not pressure.

Most children’s Mandarin apps are either cartoons with no structure or flashcard drills with no heart. HanVerse sits between the two: a real curriculum, delivered with the warmth of a friendly tutor.

Every lesson builds on the last. Vocabulary, listening, speaking and reading are woven together and reinforced by spaced review — so what children learn today, they still know next month.

How we work

A method parents can trust

01 — A structured path
