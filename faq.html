Frequently asked questions

Short answers for busy parents. Something else? andrewmunamwangi@gmail.com

What ages is HanVerse for?

HanVerse is built for children roughly ages 5–12. Lessons are short, playful and visual, with a panda companion, sounds and confetti instead of walls of text.

What is the YCT?

The Youth Chinese Test (YCT) is the official standardized Mandarin exam for school-age learners. HanVerse's four worlds — YCT 1 through YCT 4 — follow that ladder, so what your child learns maps to a recognized standard.

What if my child is a total beginner? Or already knows some Chinese?

Either way, they start with a 2-minute placement quiz. It sets their level automatically — their classroom, roadmap and daily warm-up all follow that level. No fixed starting grade, no boring review of things they know.

Is it really free?

Yes — the Free plan is $0 forever: first lessons of every YCT level, games, XP and the parents' dashboard. Paid plans ($4/month YCT Course, $10/month Premium) unlock everything and start with a 7-day free trial. See pricing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your child's account settings — no phone calls, no forms. Your child keeps Free access afterwards.

How do I follow my child's progress?

Every account includes a parents' dashboard: lessons completed, XP, streaks and recent activity — visible at a glance.

Is my child's data safe?

We collect the minimum needed to run the service, and parents can review or request deletion of their child's data at any time. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes — HanVerse runs in any modern browser on laptops, tablets and phones. Progress syncs instantly, so your child can start a lesson on the tablet and finish on your phone.

Does my child need to type Chinese?

No. Quizzes are tap-to-answer (meaning and character recognition), and speaking practice uses the microphone — no Chinese keyboard required.

We're ready — how do we start?

Create a free account, take the placement quiz, and meet HanPan. The first lesson takes about five minutes.
